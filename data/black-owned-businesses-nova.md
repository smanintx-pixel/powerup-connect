# Black-Owned Businesses in Northern Virginia — Website Directory

Enriched directory prepared November 19, 2026 for the fraternity website initiative. Every field was verified against the business's own website, its chamber/directory profile, or reputable local coverage; fields that could not be verified were left blank rather than guessed. Email is included only where the business publishes one itself.

**112 businesses** — website listed for 104, street address for 100, phone for 103, owner named for 73, published email for 31.

Businesses flagged in the Notes column should be verified before being featured on the public site. One business from the original list, Preservation Biscuit Company (Falls Church), was removed: it permanently closed in September 2026.
